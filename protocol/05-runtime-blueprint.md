- **Pre-escalation authority hook (THR-181 Track A)**: before a current manager-owned Engineering root's proposed escalation is committed, the orchestrator runs exactly one audited LLM authority evaluation of the proposed reason against the immutable, release-controlled policy `engineering/pre-escalation-authority@v1` (`runtime/orchestrator/authority_policy.py`, `runtime/orchestrator/authority.py`, wired at the `run_step_impl` escalate commit point). The policy is semantic authority only; server-owned mechanical fences are non-overridable. The evaluation snapshot carries authoritative server-derived facts with provenance (budget, lineage, active work, adverse child verdicts, partial-work evidence, permission/schema digests) and a server-PROVEN must-escalate fact (adverse verdict, partial-work evidence, DB-schema drift vs the release-pinned schema) forces ESCALATE regardless of reason prose; a permission/schema surface change during the attempt also fails closed. CONTINUE_SAME_ROOT is honored only for a byte-exact release-controlled routine phrase (`CONTINUE_ACCEPTED_REASONS`) with clean server predicates — never a prose classification — and the exact narrow permitted action is server-proven safe across every protected category; candidate identity is bound to the immutable `task_results` row id, and the final continuation CAS atomically re-validates the full fence set at consumption time. Semantic results are `ESCALATE` (existing path) and `CONTINUE_SAME_ROOT` (named clause + exact permitted action; same-root return to pending + re-enqueue). Everything else fails closed to ESCALATE. Every CONTINUE_SAME_ROOT commit atomically mints a single-use continuation envelope (`authority_continue_envelopes`, bound to candidate + immutable causal task-results row + clause + exact permitted action; `active -> consumed | violated` exactly-once, DB-enforced); while it is ACTIVE the daemon-side decision acceptance point (`_consume_completion_report` — run-step, boot sweep, zombie reaper) accepts only the exact permitted decision (`done`) — every other continued-turn decision family is audited and fails closed into the ordinary founder-escalation path without re-running the hook, and cancellation/session-failure/terminal paths spend the envelope fail-closed. Unit 2 (implemented): the continued turn's executor launch is mechanically narrowed — the launch resolver derives a turn-scoped allow set from the ACTIVE envelope identity immediately before launch (claude `--allowedTools` reduced to the single `happyranch report-completion` channel + the read/write tools needed to author the report; opencode turn-scoped permission-map swap; executors without a runtime-owned per-command allow surface — codex/pi/generic CLI/custom adapters — refuse the continued turn pre-launch into the ordinary founder-escalation lifecycle, never an unrestricted turn; baseline permission generation untouched). Full contract: [05c-orchestrator.md](05c-orchestrator.md) §THR-181.
